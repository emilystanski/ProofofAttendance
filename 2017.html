2017

Year Role Age Team Div/Tier Final Rank Location
2017 Athlete 13 Georgetown Impact 13U D3 T2 88/124 Rim Park, Waterloo
Notes: My second year of playing volleyball was vastly different than my first. For starters, I joined a different club in a different city. Although I was entering my second year of club, my mom decided it was in my best interest to play down. In the OVA, if you are born after September 1st, you can play in the age division below if it better suits your level of play. Unfortunately, at my first club, I had poor teachers who taught bad habits and outdated techniques. Although I was playing with girls who were technically a year younger than me and were first-year rep players, many of my teammates were stronger players than I was. However, it was a perfect fit for me as I landed right in the middle in terms of skill. I may have been the oldest, but I maintained being the shortest on the team.
Although I do not remember most of my first two years of playing since I was young, I remember the work I had to put in that summer after my first year in order to ensure a spot the following season. Every day, I used to go to the elementary school next to my house and teach myself how to overhand serve after being unsuccessful all season. Although I went to tryouts with a fever and the flu, I ended up accepting an offer to join Georgetown Impact.
At Prov’s, everyone received a keychain for the 10th anniversary of the Ontario Championships, and it remains one of my favourite memorabilia. However, I think the budget was spent on the keychains since it was the only item we received, but it has remained on my volleyball bags since.
