2016 Athlete 12 Selects 14U D4 T2 100/104 Rim Park, Waterloo
Notes: This was my first year playing competetive club volleyball. To be honest, I was dragged to the tryout by my mom. I remember crying to her that I didn't want to go because I was not confident enough. Little did I know that volleyball would become such a big part of my life.
I was 12 when I tried out for the 13U team for the club "Peel Selects". Because so little girls tried out, they combined the 13U and 14U team. Probably 90% of us were first year to playing club. To say we were bad, was an understatement. But now, I just look back and laugh. Unfortunatley, I dont like looking back on this time. Most of the girls didn't like me as I was one of the weakest players on the team.
2016 was my first provincials. I remeber being so intimidated to see all the teams running around this community center, and seeing all the sport courts on ice rinks with people cheering. I got a tye dye volleyball canada sweater, with stupidly my first name on the back instead of my last name. I still rocked it with no shame, but lets say I was looking forward to the next provincials. For merch from the OVA... we got a foam headband? I actually had two, but the other one broke as they were poorly made. However, I kept one to hold all my landyards next to my medals. It reminds me of where I started, and how far I've come.
